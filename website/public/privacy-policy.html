Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

AnnHub is a browser extension. You clip a selection or a whole block from a web page, or take a screenshot, and AnnHub saves it with its source into one local library where you can read, highlight, tag and export it as Markdown. There is no AnnHub account, no AnnHub server and no AI service: we operate nothing that receives your data. Section 9 covers this website.

1. What AnnHub stores, and where

  • Entries — clips (the saved Markdown text, the surrounding context for selection clips, your notes and highlights you draw while reading in the library) and screenshots (the processed image), each with its source URL, typed properties and timestamps.
  • Images — the processed screenshot bytes, exactly as you confirmed them (cropping, anonymization, mosaic and annotations applied). The unprocessed screen capture is never kept.
  • Settings — interface and capture preferences (block-entry switch and disabled sites, screenshot anonymization default, and similar).
  • Local counters — aggregate counts and timings (for example how many clips were saved this week) shown on the Settings page. They contain no page text, no URL and no title, and they are never sent anywhere.
All of this stays on your device: in your browser (IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local). The Markdown ZIP you export is an ordinary file that you own and manage.

2. The only outbound request

When you capture an element that contains a cross-origin image, the extension's background fetches that one image from the page's own site — the same request your browser makes to display it — so the element screenshot can include it. The fetch carries no credentials, accepts only public http(s) addresses, and returns only images no larger than what the library accepts. Addresses on private networks are refused. Images inside a clipped article are never downloaded; a clip keeps only their original address.

3. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

AnnHub's use of information received from Chrome extension APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The extension reads a page only when you select text, point at a block or take a screenshot, and uses that information only to provide the saving features you asked for. We do not transfer it to others, do not use it for advertising, and do not allow people to read it.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not record the pages you visit or collect your browsing history.
  • We do not collect your name, email address or any other personal information.
  • We put no analytics, tracking or telemetry in the extension, and we do not serve ads.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not load or run remote code.
  • We do not offer AI features, cloud sync or accounts.

5. Permissions explained

PermissionWhy

downloadsSave screenshots and the exported Markdown ZIP as local files.
Access to all sites (<all_urls>)Let you clip text and take screenshots on whichever page you are reading, and fetch a page's own image so an element capture can include it.

6. Security

Everything is processed locally in your browser. The extension's own pages run only the extension's own scripts; no remote code is loaded. Pages you visit cannot trigger a capture or read your library: the capture session answers only your own input.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. Delete an entry in the library (a screenshot's image goes with it), or uninstall the extension to remove the browser's copy. The Markdown ZIP you export is an ordinary file that you own and manage.

8. Children

AnnHub is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. This website

This website is a public product page. It shares no data with the extension, and it carries no ads, no cross-site tracking and no marketing scripts.

  • Visit statistics — the site measures visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, and we see totals only: page views and visits by page path (without query strings), referring site, country, browser, operating system and device type, plus page speed. It sets no cookies, uses no local storage to recognize you and does no fingerprinting. Cloudflare processes this data on our behalf and may do so outside your country.
  • Your choice — this website is delivered through Cloudflare. If Cloudflare places your IP address in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or cannot tell the country, the site asks first and loads the analytics script only after you choose “Allow”; measuring those visitors rests on their consent. The site uses the country for this decision only and does not store it. Elsewhere the script loads by default, unless your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal. Anyone can change the choice with “Analytics settings” at the bottom of the page: “Allow” starts counting right away, “Don’t allow” stops it from the next page you open. An “Allow” is asked again after 12 months.
  • What the site keeps in your browser — your analytics choice and the day you made it, in local storage, with nothing that identifies you. Outside the countries above, a session cookie that only says the site need not ask you first: it holds no country and nothing about you, and visitors in those countries never get it. And, when you pick a language other than your browser's, a session cookie that remembers it. Both cookies end when you close the browser. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what AnnHub collects or sends, we will update this page and its date before the change ships.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue in our GitHub repository.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 11 日

AnnHub 是一个浏览器扩展。你在网页里剪藏一段选区或一个整块，或者截图，AnnHub 把它连同来源存进同一个本地资料库，供你阅读、划高亮、打标签并导出为 Markdown。没有 AnnHub 账号、没有 AnnHub 服务器、没有 AI 服务：我们不运营任何接收你数据的东西。第 9 节说明本网站。

1. AnnHub 存了什么，存在哪里

  • 条目——剪藏（保存的 Markdown 正文、选区剪藏所在的语境、你的备注，以及在资料库里阅读时划出的高亮）和截图（处理后的图片），每条带着来源地址、带类型的属性和时间。
  • 图片——你确认过的截图字节（裁剪、匿名、马赛克与标注已应用）。未处理的屏幕原图不会保留。
  • 设置——界面与采集偏好（区块入口开关与已停用网站、截图匿名默认值等）。
  • 本地计数——设置页展示的聚合数字与耗时（例如本周保存了多少条剪藏）。不含页面文字、URL 和标题，也从不上传。
以上全部只留在你的设备上：在浏览器的 IndexedDB 与 chrome.storage.local 里。你导出的 Markdown ZIP 是普通文件，由你自己保管。

2. 唯一会产生的出站请求

元素截图里如果含跨域图片，扩展后台会从图片所在的站点代取那一张图——与浏览器显示它时发出的请求相同——以便元素截图能包含它。代取不带凭据，只接受公网 http(s) 地址，只返回不超过入库上限的图片，并拒绝私有网络地址。剪藏正文里的图片不会被下载，剪藏只保留它们的原地址。

3. Chrome 应用商店“有限使用”

AnnHub 对 Chrome 扩展 API 所得信息的使用，符合 Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策，包括其“有限使用”要求。扩展只在你选中文字、指向区块或截图时读取页面，且只用这些信息提供你要求的保存功能。我们不向他人转移这些信息，不用于广告，也不允许任何人阅读。

4. 我们不做什么

  • 不记录你访问的页面，不收集浏览历史。
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱等任何个人信息。
  • 扩展里没有分析、追踪或遥测，也不投放广告。
  • 不出售、不共享你的数据。
  • 不加载、不运行远程代码。
  • 不提供 AI 功能、云同步或账号。

权限用途

downloads把截图和导出的 Markdown ZIP 保存为文件。

6. 安全

一切都在你的浏览器本地处理。扩展自己的页面只执行扩展自带的脚本，不加载远程代码。你访问的页面不能触发采集，也读不到你的资料库：截图会话只响应用户本人的输入。

7. 保留与删除

数据保留到你删除为止。在资料库里删除条目（截图的图片随之删除），或卸载扩展以移除浏览器中的副本。你导出的 Markdown ZIP 是普通文件，由你自己保管。

8. 儿童

AnnHub 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集他们的信息。

9. 本网站

本网站是公开的产品介绍页，与扩展不共享任何数据；没有广告、跨站追踪或营销脚本。

  • 访问统计——网站用 Cloudflare Web Analytics 统计访问，我们只看到汇总数字：按页面路径（不含查询参数）、来源网站、国家或地区、浏览器、操作系统与设备类型分列的页面浏览与访问次数，以及页面速度。它不设置 Cookie，不用本地存储识别你，也不做指纹识别。Cloudflare 代我们处理这些数据，处理地点可能在你所在的国家以外。
  • 你的选择——本网站经由 Cloudflare 提供。如果 Cloudflare 根据你的 IP 地址判断你在欧盟、欧洲经济区、英国或瑞士，或者判断不出国家，网站会先询问，你选“允许统计”之后才加载统计脚本，对这些访客的统计以同意为依据。网站只用国家做这一判断，不保存它。其他地区默认加载，浏览器发出 Global Privacy Control 信号时除外。任何人都可以用页面底部的“统计设置”改变选择：“允许统计”立即开始统计，“不允许”从你打开的下一个页面起停止。“允许”在 12 个月后会再次询问。
  • 网站在你的浏览器里存了什么——你对统计的选择和做出选择的日期，存在本地存储里，不含任何能识别你的信息；在上述国家和地区以外，一个只表示“不必先询问”的会话 Cookie，不含国家，也不含任何关于你的信息，上述国家和地区的访客不会收到它；以及在你选择与浏览器不同的语言时，一个记住语言的会话 Cookie。两个 Cookie 都在关闭浏览器时失效。没有统计或广告 Cookie。

10. 政策变更

如果 AnnHub 收集或发送的内容发生变化，我们会在变更发布前更新本页和日期。
