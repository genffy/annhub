Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

AnnHub is a Chrome extension. You select text on a web page, or write your own idea, and AnnHub saves it as a knowledge fragment together with its source and context. There is no AnnHub account and no AnnHub server: we operate nothing that receives your data.

1. What AnnHub stores, and where

  • Fragments — the text you save, the surrounding excerpt, the page URL, host and title, where the text sits on the page, your own explanation and intended use, tags, kind and capture time.
  • Screenshots and images you capture or save, including any mosaic or blur you apply.
  • Highlights and clips — selected text, page URL and title, up to 20 characters of context, and your notes.
  • Settings — capture preferences, optional API keys or tokens, and unsent drafts so a failed save never loses what you typed.
  • Local counters — aggregate counts and timings (for example how many fragments were saved this week) shown on the Settings page. They contain no page text, no URL and no title, and they are never sent anywhere.
All of this stays on your device: in your browser (IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local).

2. The optional service that receives data

It is off by default. Data is sent only after you configure it yourself, and it goes directly from your browser to the service you chose; AnnHub does not relay it.

  • A language-model provider you choose (an OpenAI-compatible endpoint with your own API key). When you ask for a suggestion, AnnHub sends the fragment's content, the context it needs and your question. It does not send the full page, your browsing history or your other fragments, and it does not send the page URL, the page title or image files by default. A suggestion only becomes part of a fragment after you confirm it.

Each provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives. You can remove the configuration in Settings at any time; capture, saving and export keep working without them.

Separately, when you save an image from a page, the extension downloads that image from the page's own site, the same request your browser makes to display it.

3. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

AnnHub's use of information received from Chrome extension APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The extension reads a page only when you select text, capture a screenshot or press a shortcut, and uses that information only to provide the saving features you asked for. We do not transfer it to others except as described in section 2, do not use it for advertising, and do not allow people to read it.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not record the pages you visit or collect your browsing history.
  • We do not collect your name, email address or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or telemetry, and we do not serve ads.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not load or run remote code.

5. Permissions explained

PermissionWhy
storageKeep settings, drafts, highlights and clips on your device.
commandsKeyboard shortcuts for screenshots and block mode.
downloadsSave screenshots and exported Markdown ZIP files to disk.
Access to all sites (<all_urls>)Let you save text and take screenshots on whichever page you are reading, and fetch an image from its own site so the original can be saved.

6. Security

API keys live in chrome.storage.local and are readable only by the extension's own pages and service worker, never by web pages; they are not written to logs or exports. Requests to a language-model provider use HTTPS when you configure an https:// endpoint.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. Delete a fragment in the library, or uninstall the extension, to remove the browser's copy. The Markdown ZIP you export is an ordinary file that you own and manage.

8. Children

AnnHub is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change what AnnHub collects or sends, we will update this page and its date before the change ships.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue in our GitHub repository.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 7 日

AnnHub 是一个 Chrome 扩展。你在网页上选中文字，或写下自己的灵感，AnnHub 把它连同来源和语境保存为知识碎片。AnnHub 没有账号，也没有服务器：我们不运营任何接收你数据的服务。

1. 保存哪些数据，保存在哪里

  • 知识碎片——你保存的文字、所在段落摘录、网页 URL、域名和标题、文字在页面中的位置、你自己的理解与应用、标签、类型和采集时间。
  • 截图与图片——你截取或保存的图像，包括你添加的马赛克或模糊。
  • 高亮与剪藏——选中的文字、网页 URL 和标题、最多 20 个字符的上下文，以及你的备注。
  • 设置——采集偏好、可选的 API 密钥或令牌，以及尚未提交的草稿（保存失败时不丢失你输入的内容）。
  • 本地计数——聚合的次数和耗时（例如本周保存了多少条碎片），显示在设置页。其中不含网页文字、URL 或标题，也不会发送到任何地方。
这些数据都留在你自己的设备上：在浏览器里（IndexedDB 和 chrome.storage.local）。

2. 会接收数据的可选服务

它默认关闭。只有你自己配置了它，数据才会发送，并且由浏览器直接发给你选择的服务；AnnHub 不做转发。

  • 你选择的语言模型服务（带你自己 API 密钥的 OpenAI 兼容接口）。当你请求建议时，AnnHub 发送该碎片的内容、必要的上下文和你的问题；不发送完整页面、浏览历史或你的其他碎片，默认也不发送网页 URL、页面标题和图片文件。建议只有经你确认后才会成为碎片的一部分。

各服务商对收到的数据适用其自己的条款和隐私政策。你可以随时在设置页清除配置；没有它们，采集、保存和导出照常可用。

另外，当你保存网页上的图片时，扩展会从该网页自己的站点下载这张图片，与浏览器显示它时发出的请求相同。

3. Chrome 应用商店“有限使用”

AnnHub 对 Chrome 扩展 API 所得信息的使用，符合 Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策，包括其“有限使用”要求。扩展只在你选中文字、截图或按下快捷键时读取页面，且只用这些信息提供你要求的保存功能。除第 2 节所述外，我们不向他人转移这些信息，不用于广告，也不允许任何人阅读。

4. 我们不做什么

  • 不记录你访问的页面，不收集浏览历史。
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱等任何个人信息。
  • 不使用分析、追踪或遥测，不投放广告。
  • 不出售、不共享你的数据。
  • 不加载、不运行远程代码。

5. 权限说明

权限用途
storage在你的设备上保存设置、草稿、高亮和剪藏。
commands截图与区块模式的键盘快捷键。
downloads把截图和导出的 Markdown ZIP 保存为文件。
访问所有网站（<all_urls>）让你在正在阅读的任意网页上保存文字、截图，并从图片所在站点抓取原图以保存。

6. 安全

API 密钥保存在 chrome.storage.local，只有扩展自己的页面和 service worker 可以读取，网页读不到；它们不会写入日志或导出文件。当你配置 https:// 地址时，对语言模型服务的请求使用 HTTPS。

7. 保留与删除

数据保留到你删除为止。在碎片库删除碎片，或卸载扩展，即可移除浏览器中的副本。你导出的 Markdown ZIP 是一个普通文件，由你自己保管。

8. 儿童

AnnHub 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集他们的信息。

9. 政策变更

如果 AnnHub 收集或发送的内容发生变化，我们会在变更发布前更新本页和日期。

10. 联系方式

对本政策有疑问，请在我们的 GitHub 仓库提交 issue。
